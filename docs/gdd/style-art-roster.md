Style and Art Roster
Thirteen Breathing Styles, thirteen Blood Demon Art lineages and two Hybrid branches. Each has one archetype and one
signature rule, so no two play alike. Canon forms come first. Where canon left gaps, a few original forms marked [OC] fill them;
these follow the archetype rule and are never stronger than the canon forms. Canon names were checked against the Kimetsu
no Yaiba wiki's official Viz English names on Oct 16, 2026; where the anime or a later chapter renames a move, both names are
given.
