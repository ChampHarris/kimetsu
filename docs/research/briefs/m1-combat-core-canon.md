# Canon brief: M1 combat core (`m1-combat-core`)

canon-keeper pass, 2026-10-19. **Method and limits:** the Fandom wiki returned HTTP 402 to direct fetches, so wiki evidence comes from search-engine extracts of kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com pages; Viz volumes weren't checked directly. Romaji are from the wiki or a guide; kanji marked "(memory)" are unverified. Evidence only; the design is in `docs/design/specs/m1-combat-core.md`.

**Verdict: four conflicts.** (1) The Nichirin seal contradicts canon regeneration. (2) Treating only Water Surface Slash as horizontal contradicts canon, where Striking Tide, Whirlpool and Lateral Water Wheel take heads. (3) "Kata" for the light combo collides with 型 (kata), which Viz translates as "Form". (4) "Flow" for Rhythm tier 5 collides with three canon Water form names. The rest is consistent with canon or sits where canon is silent and needs an [OC] mark.

## Q2. The core rules [canon]
- **Total Concentration Breathing** (全集中の呼吸): expanding the lungs, accelerating blood flow and heartbeat; raises strength, speed and reaction time, slows poison and clots wounds. **Total Concentration: Constant** (全集中・常中) is held day and night, trained with the gourd (Ch. 50–51 / Ep. 24–25). The heartbeat link supports the Pulse as a theme. "Breath control a viewer can see" (R8-05's [canon] tag) is unverified: it rests on how the anime looks, not a cited chapter. https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Total_Concentration_Breathing
- **The Thread:** canon's **Opening Thread** (隙の糸, Suki no Ito; Viz wording unverified), a scent that points Tanjiro to an opening and lines his blade up with the neck; debut Ch. 6 / Ep. 4, and the boulder cut (Ch. 5 / Ep. 3) is told as it working. It doesn't always land (Yahaba's arrows). The mod's gold neck cue that every Slayer sees, opened by a Posture break, is an [OC] generalisation. https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Opening_Thread
- **Hard necks (Cellular Hardening):** the Hand Demon's neck muscles broke Sabito's blade (Ch. 6–8); Tanjiro's blade "doesn't even scratch Rui's neck" (Ch. 37 / Ep. 19). **The 25% Hunger gate is [OC]:** canon necks are hard by nature, not by feeding.
- **How demons die:** decapitation with a Nichirin blade or sunlight; Wisteria poison too (Shinobu). Muzan, Akaza and Kokushibo overcame decapitation (matches the second-wind list). https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Demon
- **Regeneration:** the Hand Demon regrew limbs "without slowing down" (Ch. 6–8); demons can choose to slow or stop it. https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Regeneration
- **The Nichirin seal (no regeneration for 20 s after a Nichirin wound) contradicts canon.** Canon limits regeneration-hampering to the neck (the sunlight steel stops a head regrowing), to **Bright Red** blades ("extreme regeneration-hampering properties, even on parts other than the neck") and to Yoriichi's sun wounds. Ordinary Nichirin cuts elsewhere heal at once (the Hand Demon). The GDD states the seal as a demon-core rule without calling it canon (`docs/gdd/main-gdd.md:410, 425-426`, merged as "Seal (all Nichirin)" at :60); Round 6 and D-008 pull it into M1. It needs an [OC] mark, and it gives part of the Red Blade's canon role to every blade.

## Q3. The cold-open feral demon [canon]
- Muzan killed the Kamado family while Tanjiro was away (Ch. 1 / Ep. 1); Giyu arrives after, goes for Nezuko and knocks Tanjiro out with his sword's hilt. https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_1
- Tanjiro's first demon is the Temple Demon (Ch. 2 / Ep. 2): Nezuko kicks its head off, the head lives on, the hatchet pins it, the sun ends it.
- So the cold open is [OC] built from canon pieces; fine while the player isn't Tanjiro. Its line "a hatchet won't sever a demon's neck" (`main-gdd.md:1466`) gets the detail wrong: a neck can be severed without Nichirin, it just doesn't kill.
- **The M2 turning branch (bitten, then [F] Feed) reads as turning by bite; canon allows only Muzan's blood** (or Tamayo's medicine).
- A feral, low-rank demon with swipes, a heavy, a leap, a low sweep and a grab is plausible ([OC]): new demons kill in a frenzy (Matazo); the Hand Demon grabs. **Whether fledglings feint is unverified; the GDD's Final Selection feints are [OC].**

## Q4. Naming [canon]
- **"Kata" (the 3-hit light combo):** every canon form is "N no kata" and Viz renders 型 as "Form"; the GDD's "the Kata engine" (`main-gdd.md:109`) makes it worse. A direct translation clash.
- **"Pommel Strike":** compatible; Giyu's hilt strike (Ch. 1).
- **"Flow" (Rhythm tier 5):** collides with Whirlpool, Flow (ねじれ渦・流流), Splashing Water Flow (9th) and Flowing Dance (3rd).
- Concentration Step, Velocity State, Tech, micro-clash, Recovery Breath, Posture, Rhythm, Cellular Hardening: no collision.
- "Thread": inside the GDD, the Transparent World's red cut line is also called "Threads" (`main-gdd.md:349`).

## Q5. Water's character [canon]
Flowing, versatile, the easiest style to learn; its moves imitate currents; forms suit situations (Waterfall Basin falling, Whirlpool underwater). **Canon never calls Water slow:** Drop Ripple Thrust, Curve is Tanjiro's fastest form and Dead Calm cuts at high speed, so 92 BPM is [OC] presentation and must not read as slow strikes. The shortest recovery fits the chains (Constant Flux, Striking Tide, Turbulent); the curving Step fits Flowing Dance; no i-frames is canon-silent.

## Q6. Round 6's Flow finishers ([OC]) [canon]
1st (+1.5 blocks reach, ×1.5 Posture): silent. 2nd (a second rotation ending in a launch): runs against the canon motion, one flip and one downward cut. 4th (+2 hits, the last wall-splats): matches the canon variant Striking Tide, Turbulent; credit it. 8th (a 3-block landing ring): canon's falls land in front, not in a ring.

## Q7. The M1 demon and decapitation [canon]
Slit pupils, fangs, talon-like claws, sometimes short horns (Matazo); no rank kanji in the eyes (only the Twelve Kizuki); no Blood Demon Art (arts come from feeding). Once the neck-bone is cut, head and body disintegrate into ash; no corpse is left; a head can stay aware briefly. Blessed Rain After the Drought's canon condition is a demon that willingly offers its neck.

## Second pass: the red team's demon changes (2026-10-20)
Fandom still returned HTTP 402 to every fetch, so the evidence is the first pass's wiki citations plus chapter recaps (daddyjim.com) and secondary articles (CBR, Game Rant, Fiction Horizon); anything not tied to a primary source is marked unverified.
- **The guard stance between strings: [OC], not how canon shows feral demons fight.** No low-rank demon in canon blocks body hits (the Final Selection demons, the Temple Demon, the Swamp Demon, Matazo); they lean on regeneration. Canon's demons protect the neck: the Hand Demon wrapped hardened limbs around its head and neck (secondary source, chapter unverified), its neck broke Sabito's blade, and Rui hardens his (Ch. 37). Raised arms read as Akaza's martial arts (Ch. 63). The canon-shaped tell is forearms crossed over the neck that take the cut and visibly regrow; it previews the Hand Demon's M2 neck arms (`docs/gdd/boss-phase-scripts.md:49–51`), so it's Champ's call. It must never look like a white deflect.
- **Super armour on committed strikes: canon-plausible [OC].** Demons press on through wounds that close at once (the Hand Demon, Ch. 6–8; Akaza under Kyojuro's blade, Ch. 63). From M3, a hit that severs the striking limb should end that limb's strike (`main-gdd.md:423`).
- **Regeneration:** the 20 s seal contradicts canon (first pass). A 10 s pause after any wound contradicts it the same way: canon's demons regrow while they're being cut, and the canon slowers are sunlight (and Nichirin's sunlight-steel at the neck, Bright Red blades, sun wounds), Wisteria in large doses, Muzan's will and Tamayo's drugs (finale; chapter unverified). In M1 the pause is also the seal under another label, since every mod wound is a Nichirin hit. Regeneration at once is [canon]; paying for it in Hunger is canon-consistent [OC] (regeneration "depletes energy" and eating restores strength, CBR, secondary; Nezuko sleeps to recover) and is the GDD's own rule (`main-gdd.md:410, 421–422`); Posture recovery falling with damage taken is [OC], canon silent. Canon never shows an Upper Moon tiring, so the attrition reading fits a fledgling only. The Merge Log's reason for cutting the Sun-Steel Tsuba (`merge-log.md:77–78`) applies to any blanket stop on regeneration.
- **The vanilla damage floor: consistent, [OC].** Only Nichirin and the sun kill: Nezuko kicked the Temple Demon's head off and it lived until sunrise (Ch. 2). Canon is silent on fire, lava and falls.
- **The Hardening clang's crit damage contradicts canon** (`main-gdd.md:128–129`): a hardened neck takes no cut at all (Sabito's blade broke; Tanjiro's didn't scratch Rui's, Ch. 37). Mark it [OC] at least; Champ's call.
- **The arena at night:** sunlight kills (`main-gdd.md:410`), so the grey box must keep it out (time held at night), not just start at night.
- **Names:** "decapitation" in the death rules should read "Nichirin decapitation" (a non-Nichirin beheading doesn't kill); "heavy armour" isn't a glossary term (the glossary's is super armour). No canon collisions in "guard stance", "regeneration pause", "strike armour" or "fledgling" (the GDD's word, `main-gdd.md:414`).
- **Unverified:** the chapter of the Hand Demon wrapping its arms around its neck; the chapters for Nezuko's sleep and Tamayo's drugs; whether Gyomei's weapons are Nichirin; Viz wording.
